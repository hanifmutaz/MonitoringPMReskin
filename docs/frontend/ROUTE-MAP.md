## Notes

- **"PM Monthly" / "PM Weekly" naming**: the product map in `01-PRODUCT-UX-BRIEF.md` treats these as distinct modules; the codebase implements both under a single `pm-line` route family with Weekly/Monthly presumably distinguished by data or a query param **inside** `PmLineStatusPage`/`PmLineFormPage`/`PmLineHistoryPage` (`Unknown` — not yet confirmed, needs a direct read). Do not split this into two route families without confirming the actual internal structure first — it may already be handled as one page with a mode switch, which would be a legitimate existing pattern to preserve.
- **Roles & Permissions and Audit Log** are the two clearest gaps between the product map and the verified route tree. Both are marked `Unknown`, not invented — flagged as blocking open questions rather than assumed to need new routes.
